    Field Guide

    I'm not a gamer. Is this for me?

    Yes. Half the fun is the chat, the commentary and the chaos. Watch along, play Jackbox from your phone, and cheer for whoever's losing.

    Do I have to donate?

    No. But it's the reason we do this. Donations go straight to Children's National Hospital through Extra Life, and even $5 counts.

    How do I play?

    Sign up above and pick your events. Smash runs through Battle Arenas, Jackbox through a room code on stream, and Fortnite as squads. Match times and the Discord invite come by email.

    What do I need?

    A Switch with Smash Ultimate and Nintendo Switch Online for the Smash events, a phone for Jackbox, and Fortnite on anything it runs on. Discord for voice.
