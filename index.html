    Field Guide

    I'm not a gamer. Should I still come?

    Yes. Half the party is here for the snacks, the board games and the people. There's always a couch, a card game and someone to chat with.

    Do I have to donate?

    No. But it's the reason we do this. Donations go straight to Children's National Hospital through Extra Life, and even $5 counts.

    Will I be on camera?

    Only if you want to be. The stream desk and Smash arena are on camera; the lounge and tabletop area are camera-free. Just let AJ know if you'd rather stay off stream.

    Can I play from home?

    Absolutely. Remote players get scheduled slots in the Smash Championship, friendslop, casual Jackbox and the late Fortnite session. Register ahead so we can save you a seat.

    What should I bring?

    Just yourself. If you have a favorite Switch controller, bring it; it helps during the tournament. Food and drinks are covered.

    What's the deal with quiet hour?

    Around 7:30 the littlest party member goes to bed. We turn the volume down for about an hour, keep the upstairs off-limits, and let AJ step away. After that, the house party continues while the stream moves to Fortnite.

    Where is it?

    At AJ's place. The address comes with your RSVP, not on the public site.
